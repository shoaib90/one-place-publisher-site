Privacy policy

Last updated 13 November 2026

One-Place Publisher is a private tool used by one person, its owner, to publish videos to the owner's own YouTube channels. It has no other users. This policy explains what it accesses and what it does with it.

What it accesses

  • To sign in to the app: the Google account's email address and basic profile, used only to check that the person signing in is the owner.
  • For each connected YouTube channel: the channel's ID, name and picture, its playlists, and permission to upload videos, set thumbnails, add videos to playlists and upload captions.

How it uses that data

Only to do what the owner asks: upload the owner's videos to the owner's channels at the chosen times, and confirm they went live. It does not read or change other videos, comments, messages, analytics or account settings.

Storage and security

  • The login permission Google gives each channel is stored encrypted, on a server only the owner controls. No passwords are stored.
  • Video, thumbnail and captions files are kept in private storage only while they are needed for publishing.
  • The app keeps a log of what it published and what YouTube replied, so the owner can see what happened.

Sharing

Data is not sold, shared, or used for advertising, and is not shared with anyone except Google/YouTube itself, as needed to publish. There are no analytics or tracking.

Google API Services User Data Policy

One-Place Publisher's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Disconnecting a channel in the app deletes its stored login and revokes it at Google. Access can also be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions about this policy can go to the support email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.

One-Place Publisher is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google or YouTube.
