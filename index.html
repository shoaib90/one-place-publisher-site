One-Place Publisher

A private publishing tool, used by one person.

One-Place Publisher is a personal tool its owner uses to upload and schedule finished videos to their own YouTube channels from one screen, instead of going to each channel separately. Support for the owner's own Instagram account and, later, LinkedIn and X is planned.

Who can use it

Only its owner. There is no public sign-up, and the app refuses every Google account except the owner's. The only YouTube channels it ever connects to are the owner's.

What it does with YouTube

  • Uploads a video to the owner's channel as private, with the publish time the owner chose.
  • Sets that video's thumbnail, adds it to a playlist, and uploads its captions.
  • Checks that the video went public at its time.

It does not read, change or delete any other videos, comments or settings.

How it handles data is described in the privacy policy.

One-Place Publisher is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google or YouTube.
